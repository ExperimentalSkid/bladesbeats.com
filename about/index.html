About BladesBeats.

A music story that began in Oslo and found its next chapter in Sevilla.

BladesBeats is a Norwegian DJ and producer based in Sevilla, making electronic music for late nights, moving rooms, and the people in them.

OriginOslo, Norway
Current baseSevilla, Spain
LanguagesEnglish / Spanish

The first chapter began in Holmlia, Oslo, in the early 2010s. Youth and community spaces including BUSH and Café Condio offered studio access, a creative circle, and the first real chance to turn curiosity into music.

After moving to Sevilla in 2017, the project went quiet for a few years. Beatmaking and sessions with vocalists brought the spark back, followed by a deeper move into electronic music.

Regular studio work with Manuel Ávila / Baster Beats gave that new direction room to grow through original tracks and remixes.

In 2025, DJ training with Quini Rivera at Impulsa Music Center brought the studio and booth closer together: technique, transitions, and a stronger feel for the room.

Today, BladesBeats brings those threads together across original releases, remixes, DJ sets, and live appearances—music built around energy, movement, and connection.

Based in Sevilla and comfortable in English or Spanish, BladesBeats welcomes messages about gigs and collaborations through the contact page.
